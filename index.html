Biography

Rui is a Research Associate at the Centre for AI and Decision Sciences, Alliance Manchester Business School, University of Manchester. Her research sits at the intersection of decision science, network science, AI and machine learning, simulation, and operations research.

Rui has a PhD in Business and Management from the University of Manchester. Her doctoral research developed interpretable data-driven approaches to examine how social influence, information diffusion and network structures shape individual and collective decision-making.

Rui's current research applies computational modelling to complex supply-chain and logistics systems, with particular interests in agent-based modelling, machine learning, network analysis, multimodal logistics and transparent decision support.
