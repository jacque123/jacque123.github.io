Four connected research themes

My work combines analytical modelling, simulation and data-driven methods to study how decisions and interactions shape complex systems.

Complex Systems & Network Science

Network structure, information diffusion, social influence, resilience and interconnected systems.

Heterogeneous actors, behavioural rules, bargaining, collaboration and emergent system dynamics.

Multimodal transport, routing, decarbonisation, collaboration and digital-twin inspired decision support.
